# Sources and licensing

Status: Approved

Shakespeer is non-commercial and redistributes texts and reference works under their licenses.
Every piece of sourced content says where it came from and under what terms.

## Requirements

- **ATR-001** — Every source in the corpus MUST be described in the source registry
  ([CRP-080](../data/corpus.md)): name, description, home page, license name and license URL,
  and the exact attribution text the license requires.
- **ATR-002** — The reader MUST show, at the end of each version's text, an "About this text"
  section: the version's source(s), the license with a link, and the attribution text.
- **ATR-003** — The overflow menu MUST offer **About this text**, opening the same information
  in a dialog.
- **ATR-010** — Every sourced definition MUST show its source's short name, linked to a dialog
  with the full source information and license link.
- **ATR-011** — Own and imported notes show "You" or the collection name as origin (PNL-011),
  distinct from a corpus source.
- **ATR-020** — The app MUST have an "About & licenses" page (linked from play selection)
  listing every source and license, and the app's own license.
- **ATR-021** — A source whose license forbids commercial use (e.g. CC BY-NC) MUST be marked as
  such in the registry, so a future change in the project's status can be checked mechanically.
